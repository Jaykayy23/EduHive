## Factual product description

EduHive is a web-based student community and study-tool codebase. It combines posts, comments, bookmarks and messaging with HiveQ document/text-to-quiz workflows and a study tutor. The public `/hiveq/demo` demonstrates fixed synthetic samples with answering, explanations, scoring, reset and export; it does not perform live AI generation.

The stack is Next.js/React/TypeScript, PostgreSQL/Prisma and a separate Python/FastAPI backend. Live integrations include Gemini for HiveQ, NVIDIA Build for the tutor, Resend email, Stream Chat and UploadThing storage. Buyers configure their own accounts and pay provider/hosting usage. Authentication, moderation and ownership checks protect user operations. No billing/subscriptions, multi-school tenancy or mobile app is provided in this scope.
